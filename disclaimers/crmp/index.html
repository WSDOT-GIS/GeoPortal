The purpose of this map is to display the County Road Mileposts that have been established by the counties, however this does not always infer County ownership as some county roads have been annexed and become city-owned.

When zoomed to a county, the County Road Numbers will appear, and using the CRMP Tool, when clicking on a road that has a County Road Number a County Road Milepost will be displayed.
